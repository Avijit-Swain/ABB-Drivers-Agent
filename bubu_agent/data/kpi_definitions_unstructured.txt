 KPI Definitions for Driver Analysis Chatbot
Purpose
This PDF is the unstructured KPI-definition source for the Driver Analysis Chatbot. Use it when the user asks what a KPI
means, gives a misspelled KPI name, or describes a KPI indirectly. For selected drivers, elasticities, ranges, forecasts,
contribution, and historical trends, use the structured Excel knowledge base.
Recommended lookup behavior
First search by exact KPI name. If no exact match is found, use fuzzy matching and synonyms. If the user describes the
concept, use semantic search over the definition and business meaning. When confidence is low, ask a confirmation
question such as: "Did you mean Consumer Price Index?"
Data Center / Hyperscaler
Category
Sector demand indicator
Definition
Measures demand, investment, or activity related to data centers and hyperscale infrastructure.
Business meaning
Higher data center activity may indicate stronger electrification, power, grid, automation, and
infrastructure demand. In the demo, this is treated as a selected driver for ELSP.
Possible synonyms /
user terms
hyperscaler, data center growth, cloud infra, server infrastructure, AI data center demand
US GDP
Category
Macro indicator
Definition
Gross Domestic Product of the United States. It measures the total economic output of the US economy.
Business meaning
Higher GDP generally indicates stronger economic activity and can support business demand. Users may
ask this as US economy, macro growth, or economic output.
Possible synonyms /
user terms
US economy, economic growth, GDP growth, macro growth, output of US economy
China GDP
Category
Macro indicator
Definition
Gross Domestic Product of China. It measures the total economic output of China.
Business meaning
Higher China GDP can indicate stronger economic activity and broader demand conditions in China.
Possible synonyms /
user terms
China economy, Chinese growth, macro growth China, China output
China IIP
Category
Industrial indicator
Definition
Index of Industrial Production for China. It measures changes in industrial output such as manufacturing,
mining, and utilities.
Business meaning
Higher IIP indicates stronger industrial activity and may increase demand for industrial products.
Possible synonyms /
user terms
China industrial production, manufacturing output, industrial activity, China factory output

Europe Producer Price Index
Category
Price/inflation indicator
Definition
Measures changes in prices received by producers for goods and services in Europe.
Business meaning
It reflects producer-level inflation and cost pressure, which may influence demand, pricing, and
investment decisions.
Possible synonyms /
user terms
Europe PPI, producer prices, industrial inflation, price index, producer-level prices
Copper Price
Category
Commodity indicator
Definition
Market price of copper. Copper is a key industrial metal used in electrical equipment, cables, and
infrastructure.
Business meaning
Copper price can reflect commodity cost pressure as well as industrial demand conditions. A higher price
may create cost pressure in some contexts.
Possible synonyms /
user terms
copper, copper commodity, metal price, copper index, copper inflation
